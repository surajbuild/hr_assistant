Code of Conduct and Business Travel Policy
Version 2.1 — Effective 1 April 2026

PART A — CODE OF CONDUCT

1. Professional Behaviour
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients and vendors with respect. Harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind is not tolerated and must be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.

2. Dress Code
Business casual attire is the standard dress code from Monday to Thursday. Fridays are casual dress days. Client-facing meetings require formal attire.

3. Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, directorship or financial interest in a vendor or competitor to HR within 7 days.

4. Gifts
Employees may accept gifts from business partners only if the value is below INR 2,000. Gifts above this value must be declared to HR.

PART B — BUSINESS TRAVEL

5. Travel Approval
All business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before booking. International travel additionally requires approval from the department head.

6. Daily Allowance
Employees travelling for business within India receive a daily allowance of INR 2,500. For international travel the daily allowance is USD 75.

7. Accommodation
Hotel bookings must be made through the approved travel desk at least 5 working days in advance. The maximum hotel limit is INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night elsewhere.

8. Expense Claims
Travel expense claims, with original receipts, must be submitted within 15 days of returning from the trip. Claims submitted later than 30 days will not be reimbursed.
