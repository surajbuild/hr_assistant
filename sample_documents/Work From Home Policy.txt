Work From Home Policy
Version 1.0 — Effective 1 January 2026

1. Purpose
This policy defines when and how employees may work remotely while keeping productivity, security and team collaboration high.

2. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period are eligible to request work from home (WFH). Interns and employees serving a notice period are not eligible unless approved by HR.

3. WFH Allowance
Eligible employees may work from home for up to 8 days per calendar month. Unused WFH days do not carry forward to the next month. WFH on Mondays and Fridays requires prior approval from the reporting manager.

4. How to Apply
WFH requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 1 working day in advance. Emergency WFH requests (illness in the family, transport disruption) may be raised on the same day before 9:30 AM.

5. Working Hours and Availability
Employees working from home must follow standard office hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM), stay reachable on the company chat tool, and attend all scheduled meetings with video enabled where possible.

6. Equipment and Expenses
The company provides a laptop for remote work. Internet expenses are reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per month on submission of bills. Personal devices must not be used to store company data.

7. Data Security
Employees must connect through the company VPN, lock their screen when away, and must not work from public Wi-Fi networks without the VPN.

8. Attendance
WFH days are marked with the status "Work From Home" and count as present days. Late login rules (grace period of 15 minutes after 9:00 AM) apply equally to WFH days.

9. Policy Violations
Repeated non-availability during working hours or misuse of WFH may lead to withdrawal of WFH eligibility for up to 3 months.
